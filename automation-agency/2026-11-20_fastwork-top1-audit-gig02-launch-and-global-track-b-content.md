## 2. Problem → Architecture & Solution

### Pain Points
1. **Marketplace Invisibility:** Auditing the live Fastwork profile exposed concrete friction points — placeholder bio text, missing experience/skills fields, and gig titles lacking high-intent search terms (`n8n`, `Make`, `Apps Script`, `LINE OA`).
2. **Single-Narrow-Offer Bottleneck:** Having only a lead-alert gig missed SME buyers looking for broader custom workflow automation across LINE OA, Sheets, Docs, and AI summarization.
3. **Content Model Confusion:** Copying B2C AI-agency course sellers (hype hooks, Skool funnels) attracts beginners instead of B2B buyers and engineering hiring managers.

### Architecture & Execution
1. **Fastwork Top 1% Competitive Audit (`04-Competitive-Analysis/fastwork_top1_competitive_analysis.md`):**
   - Benchmarked 7 top sellers across `888` AI Automation listings (`438` for `n8n`, `686` for `Chatbot`).
   - Extracted 4 recurring 5-star review drivers: (1) jargon-free explanations for non-technical owners, (2) fast business-logic comprehension, (3) proactive debugging/updates, and (4) 100% client ownership of scripts/systems with no recurring lock-in.
2. **Gig #1 `v2.2` Upgrade & Gig #2 Custom AI Automation Launch:**
   - **Gig #1 (`v2.2`):** Added high-intent search keywords (`n8n/Make/LINE`), introduced a `฿500` 1:1 Consult entry tier to accelerate early verified reviews, and rendered 2 new `2400×1350` HTML→PNG gallery slides (`gallery_gig01_engineering_proof` highlighting `51/51` tests + `gallery_gig01_packages_v2`).
   - **Gig #2 (`Submitted`):** Built the full consultative offer (`฿2,900` Starter / `฿4,900` Pro + AI / `฿8,900` Full System), 15 search tags, sales/scope guardrails, and 3 `2400×1350` Minimalist Editorial visuals (`cover_gig02_v1`, `gallery_gig02_packages`, `gallery_gig02_process`) verified against `3:2` and `185px` mobile safe zones.
3. **Global Track B Content Strategy (`04-Competitive-Analysis/top1_global_ai_agency_content_analysis.md`):**
   - Separated **Track A (B2C course/community sellers)** from **Track B (B2B Proof-of-Work Operators / GTM Engineers)**.
   - Adopted a lean **"1 Build → 3 Proof Assets"** cadence (`<= 2–3 hrs/week`): 1 English LinkedIn post/week (Architecture Diagram / 45-sec Demo / Scars & Debug post, external links in first comment only), 1 English YouTube technical teardown every 2 weeks (`5–8 min`), and X cross-posting — keeping `90%` of weekly bandwidth protected for full-time job search (`P1.1`).
